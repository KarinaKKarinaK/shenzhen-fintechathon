# Zishi 自食其力, the robot that pays for itself

Deck text, ten slides, under ten minutes. One idea per slide. Numbers in yuan.

## 1. Title
Zishi 自食其力. The robot that pays for itself.
A wallet for an agricultural drone on hire purchase. Shenzhen FinTechathon 2026, Topic F.
Live: zishi-fintechathon.vercel.app

## 2. The pilot
Over 200,000 agricultural drone pilots in China. A drone costs about 55,000 yuan. Spraying pays 3 to 15 yuan per mu.
A pilot with nothing to pledge cannot get a loan. The drone sits in a showroom. The field waits.

## 3. What Zishi does
The drone earns per mu sprayed, confirmed by the farmer who pays.
Each fee repays the lender, fills a bond and leaves the rest to the pilot.
At zero the title moves on chain. The drone belongs to the person who flew it.

## 4. One task, seven steps
Plan, lock, check, work, confirm, repay, own. Each step is an event mirrored from the contracts.
Live demo: five moments in three minutes. Job declined, instalment paid, faked log held, slash by proof, title transferred.

## 5. Money decisions are made in code, never by the model
Four permission tiers. The model reads and drafts. The machine key pays inside caps and allowlists. The operator's physical button holds the top.
Fourteen rules in the policy engine, checked on every signed intent. Every number from a tool.

## 6. Two bonds, slashed only by proof
Pilot bond, earned from fees, slashed for the pilot's own cheating.
Vendor bond, 5,000 yuan posted by us, slashed for the agent's mistakes. The pilot never pays for the AI.
A slash needs a signed intent in the log that breaks a rule the contract can check. Only a party of the job can submit. No trusted judge.

## 7. Holds under attack
52 attack cases, 52 blocked. 7 benign messages, 7 passed. A naive wallet falls to 51 of 52.
Try your own on the evidence page. The live wallet never moves.

## 8. The economics
Drone 55,000 yuan, repaid over about 13,750 mu. Instalment 20 to 45 percent of each fee. Starting floor 200 yuan, bond minimum 2,000.
The title protects the lender. The bonds protect the counterparties from misbehaviour. The number the lender watches is repayment per mu.

## 9. Honest about what is real
Four contracts compiled and tested, 40 Foundry tests passing. Engine mirrors every rule.
Chain simulated and labelled until a funded key is set, then every event is anchored on Base Sepolia with explorer links. Telemetry signed by our keys in the demo; a secure element on the drone in production. A licensed lessor in a sandbox, not a bank.

## 10. The agricultural drone is the first
The same rail works for any machine that does paid work someone can verify. Delivery drones per drop, robot arms per part, robotaxis per trip.
Next: one lessor pilot with ten drones, real flight logs, WeBank's FISCO BCOS as the production chain.
