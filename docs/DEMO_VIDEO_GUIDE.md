## 01 Recommended Structure

### Project and team introduction

0:00 to 0:27. The problem (a pilot with nothing to pledge cannot finance a 55,000 yuan drone), Zishi in one sentence, Topic F, and the team: Karina Kalicka-Molin, Joshua Khoo, Julian D Mundelein.

### End-to-end demonstration of the core scenario

0:27 to 2:50. One drone on the live site, settling on the deployed contracts on FISCO BCOS: a job declined by the reserve rule, the pilot asking the agent why (clarifying question, correction, refusal, in English and 中文), a paid job split by the JobBoard and Lease contracts, a faked flight log held, a compromised intent slashed by proof from the vendor bond, the title moving to the pilot at zero, and the wallet recovering from a failed call with a refund by contract.

### Safety mechanisms, innovation and results

2:50 to 4:00. The same job on Base Sepolia's public explorer, `pnpm verify:all` re-checking 329 of 329 receipts, the attack console with honest per layer numbers, random actor keys, the operator button behind a token, the front running fix, and WeBank's stack: FISCO BCOS, FATE federated credit and the model fingerprint on chain.

## 02 Video Quality

### Use 16:9 landscape and preferably 1080p

Screen recording at 1920 by 1080, browser at 100 percent zoom, dark theme off, one window.

### Make interface text, narration and English subtitles clearly legible

Narration in English at a calm pace (about 140 words a minute); English subtitles burned in from the script; key numbers zoomed when shown.

## 03 Final Check

### No copyrighted or sensitive material

Only our own interface, code and synthetic data; no real farmer or pilot data; no third party music; no keys or tokens on screen (the demo token is entered before recording).

### Suggested filename: Track-TeamName-DemoVideo

`International-Zishi-DemoVideo.mp4`, under five minutes.
